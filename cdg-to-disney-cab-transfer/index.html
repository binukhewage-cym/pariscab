CDG to Disney Cab Transfer

Your Private Cab from CDG to Disneyland Paris

Landing at Charles de Gaulle with a family in tow is tiring enough without working out how to reach Disneyland Paris. Which train, which platform, where the shuttle leaves from? Disney Paris Prestige Transfer offers a CDG to Disney cab transfer that takes all of that off your plate.

You book a car and a price before you leave home. When you land, your driver is already in the arrivals hall holding a sign with your name. They take the bags, you walk to the car, and about 45 minutes later you’re at your hotel entrance. No timetable to catch, no changing trains and no sharing the vehicle with strangers.

Disney Paris Prestige Transfer does one route well: Charles de Gaulle to Disneyland Paris and back again.

Everything You Need, Nothing You Don't

Real-Time Flight Tracking

We monitor your flight from the moment it departs. If your arrival is delayed by thirty minutes or two hours, your private chauffeur adjusts your airport transfer automatically. You will never be charged for flight delay waiting time.

Meet & Greet at Arrivals

Your personal chauffeur will be in the arrivals hall with your name displayed. No phone calls trying to describe where you are standing, no circling the pickup zone in a taxi. You walk out, you see your name, and you go.

Fixed Prices, Confirmed Upfront

Every booking comes with a fixed price agreed in advance. What we quote is what you pay – regardless of traffic, time of day, or how long the journey takes. You will never get to find your fare has changed.

Complimentary Water & Free Wi-Fi

  • Every vehicle carries complimentary bottled water and offers free onboard WiFi as standard. If you have a specific preference – a particular temperature in the car – let your skilled chauffeur know when you book.

Why Book a Private CDG to Disneyland Paris Cab

The price is settled before you fly. There’s no meter. The fare you accept online is the fare you pay, and it covers tolls and your standard luggage.

Your flight sets the pick-up time. We follow your flight live. If you arrive early, we’re there. If you land two hours late, nothing changes and you aren’t charged for it.

You walk out to a driver, not a queue. The taxi rank at CDG can be long after a big arrival. Your driver is waiting inside, so you skip it.

One car for the whole group. The price is for the vehicle, not per person, so it often works out cheaper than four train tickets or four shuttle seats once you count the last stretch to the hotel.

Door-to-door. The train and the shuttle leave you at a station or a stop. We take you to your hotel’s main entrance.

Real help with the bags. Your driver loads and unloads, and folds the stroller into the boot. After a flight with small children, that matters more than it sounds.

Which CDG Terminal? We Cover All of Them

Charles de Gaulle has three terminal areas: Terminal 1, Terminal 2 (halls 2A to 2G) and Terminal 3. Tell us your terminal and flight number when you book and your driver will be at the right exit.

If you’re not sure, your flight number is enough. We check it and confirm the terminal with you before you travel.

The TGV is very fast, and if a departure lines up with your flight and you’re travelling light, it’s a good option. But trains are limited each day, and you still need to get from the station to your hotel. The RER is the cheapest, but it means two trains and two sets of stairs with your luggage. For families, groups and late arrivals, a private cab is usually the calmer choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get from CDG to Disneyland Paris by cab?

It usually takes 40 to 50 minutes to get from CDG to Disneyland Paris by cab. At rush hour or in heavy traffic, it can take 60 minutes or more.

Where will I meet my driver?

In the arrivals hall of your terminal, holding a sign with your name. You’ll also get your driver’s contact details before you land.

Can you take us to our hotel directly?

Yes. We drop you at the entrance of your Disneyland Paris hotel or partner hotel, or at any address you give us.

Can I book a return transfer from Disneyland Paris to CDG?

Yes. Book your arrival and your departure together, or add the return later.

Is a private cab cheaper than a taxi?

Often. A taxi meter rises with traffic and surcharges, while our fare is fixed in advance, so you always know the cost.

Do you work at night?

Yes, we’re available 24 hours a day.
